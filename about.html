About EduTrack

EduTrack is an AI-powered student performance and attendance management system designed to help educational institutions efficiently monitor student activities, analyze academic progress, and improve learning outcomes.

Our Objective

The main objective of EduTrack is to develop a smart platform that enables institutions to manage attendance, track academic performance, and provide personalized learning support using Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, and automation.

Technologies Used

🌐 Full Stack Web

Responsive frontend and backend application for student management.

📊 Power BI Analytics

Interactive dashboards to analyze attendance and performance.

🤖 Generative AI

AI chatbot to assist students with learning guidance.

⚙️ Automation

n8n workflows for alerts and automated reports.
